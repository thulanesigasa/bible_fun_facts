Data Protection & Security

Hardware-Backed Privacy Architecture

Your reflections, private prayer notes, and scripture insights belong exclusively to you. exégeomai is built with zero advertising trackers and zero commercial telemetry.

Security & Encryption Standards
Compliant with Google Play Data Safety, GDPR, and Android Keystore standards

exégeomai guarantees that sensitive auth tokens, passwords, and study reflections never touch unencrypted storage. Our transparent chunking engine stores cryptographic keys directly inside the device's hardware security module (Secure Element / TEE).

AES-256-CBC Journal
FIPS 197 compliant symmetric encryption for study journal exports. Sealed with SHA-256 MAC for cryptographic tamper detection.
Kotlin FLAG_SECURE
Prevents operating system task manager previews, external screen recorders, and accidental screenshots from capturing private notes.
Salted SHA-256 PIN
4-digit hardware fallback with exponential rate-limiting lockouts (30 seconds after 5 failed attempts; 5 minutes after 10).
Google Play & GDPR Compliance

Account & Data Deletion Portal

In compliance with Google Play Store policies and GDPR requirements, users have the right to permanently purge their account and all associated data either directly in the app or via this web portal.

Request Account & Data Purge

When you request account deletion, our PostgreSQL backend executes the delete_user_account() cascade function, which permanently purges your authentication record, user profile, favorite scriptures, reading streaks, and storage avatars. This action is irreversible.

Immediate In-App Purge: You can delete your account instantly inside the mobile app by navigating to Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account & Purge Data.
Request Received: Your deletion request has been submitted to the compliance officer. A verification link has been dispatched to your email address to confirm identity before executing the permanent database cascade.
