Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026 • Version 1.0.4

Your spiritual reflections, study journal, and personal privacy are sacred to us. This Privacy Policy details our data minimization policies, encryption standards, and user control rights under GDPR, CCPA, and POPIA.

1. Our Foundational Privacy Pledge

At exégeomai, we believe your walk in Scripture should be completely free from commercial tracking, behavioral profiling, and advertising algorithms:

  • Zero Advertising Trackers: We do not integrate advertising networks, monetization SDKs, or data broker scripts.
  • We Never Sell Your Data: Your profile, study notes, reading history, and spiritual reflections are never sold, rented, or bartered.
  • Strict Data Minimization: We only collect what is strictly necessary to provide authenticated sync, study bookmarks, and offline features.

2. Categories of Information Collected

  • Account Details: Email address, display name, username, and encrypted credentials managed via Supabase Auth.
  • Study Progress: Saved scriptures, favorites, daily devotionals completed, and reading streak metrics stored locally and synchronized securely.
  • Security Preferences: Biometric preferences, 4-digit PIN hashes (salted SHA-256), and inactivity timeout preferences stored in hardware-backed Android Keystore and iOS Keychain.

3. Offline Storage & Local Processing

All 14,298 Strong's Hebrew and Greek lexical entries and offline Bible translations reside directly on your device in an embedded SQLite database. Search queries, lexical indexing, and Bible readings are processed locally on your device with zero telemetry transmitted.

4. Self-Service Account Deletion (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

You have the absolute right to purge your data at any time. Under Profile > Settings > Delete Account, you can trigger complete self-service erasure. This permanently deletes your profile from Supabase auth.users, clears all favorites, notes, and progress records, and purges all local storage.

5. Crash Telemetry & Diagnostic Information

We utilize Sentry crash reporting solely for capturing unhandled runtime errors, native exceptions, and performance bottlenecks to ensure stability across Android and iOS hardware. All authentication headers and sensitive credentials are automatically stripped prior to transmission.
